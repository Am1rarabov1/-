FRONT PAGE — OUR POSITION

Low voltage, high craft.

Every building has two electrical systems. One carries power and gets all the credit. The other carries meaning — the data, the cameras, the music, the quiet intelligence of a room that knows you have come home. This paper is devoted to the second system, and to the firm that installs it with monastic precision: Smart Installers.

We believe a cable run is an argument, and that sloppy arguments fail inspection. We believe racks should be photographed like architecture. We believe the difference between 98% and 99.98% uptime is a moral one. Turn the page; we will prove all three.
