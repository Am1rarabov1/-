How Maison was made

Maison is part of the Atelier Collection — three sister sites to Atelier built by Claude (Fable 5), each keeping the dusk-photography soul while pushing the scroll choreography much further. Maison's idea: the website is a walking tour of a house, and scrolling is your feet.

i.The doors open

The site begins behind a pair of porcelain panels that part like double doors — a brass rule draws, MAISON tightens its letterspacing, and the veil splits with xPercent: ±101 and a heavy power4.inOut. First impressions are architectural on purpose: you enter this site the way you enter a house.

ii.The tour: one pin, three mask wipes

The centerpiece is a full-viewport section pinned for 340% of scroll while four rooms exchange places, each with a different clip-path wipe:

  • Circle iris into the library — circle(0% → 75%), like an eye adjusting.
  • Vertical blind sweep into the bath — inset(0 100% 0 0 → 0 0 0 0).
  • Arch reveal into the dining room — an inset with a huge top border-radius that rises like a doorway, then relaxes to full-bleed.

Every room image also runs a slow continuous zoom (scale 1.12 → 1) across the whole pin, so nothing on screen is ever perfectly still. A glass caption panel rewrites itself per room and side-dots track the sequence.

iii.Everything else that moves

  • An intent paragraph that rises line by line from masked containers (SplitText lines), beside a brass rule that draws itself.
  • A drifting italic marquee whose speed follows scroll velocity.
  • Detail cards on three different parallax speeds (data-speed), so the grid breathes as you pass.
  • Counters with drifting numerals in the dark "In Measure" band.
  • A finale image whose frame morphs from rectangle to arch on scroll while the photo counter-parallaxes inside it.
  • A brass progress hairline across the very top of the page.

iv.The stack & the recipe

  • Static HTML, GSAP 3 + ScrollTrigger + SplitText, Lenis. No frameworks, no build.
  • Marcellus for engraved display caps, Cormorant italic for the voice, Karla for body.
  • Photography and the hero cinemagraph generated with Higgsfield (nano banana pro + Kling 3.0 Turbo), all to one dusk lighting brief.
  • To repeat: pick a physical journey (a tour), pin one stage, and give each transition its own mask. Variety lives in the wipes, not in the easing — the easing stays calm everywhere.
  • Everything degrades to a clean static read under prefers-reduced-motion.
