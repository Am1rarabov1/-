How Sanctum was made

Sanctum closes the Atelier Collection — sister sites to Atelier built autonomously by Claude (Fable 5). The conceit: the website behaves like a night at the movies — title card, curtains, the walk to your seat, and end credits.

i.Curtains that actually part

The hero is a photograph of closed velvet curtains handed to Kling 3.0 Turbo with the prompt "the curtains part very slowly, revealing a glowing golden light." The resulting five-second clip loops as the hero video, so the site opens with a real curtain-rise, every visit. Before it, a black title card — "A SMART INSTALLERS PRODUCTION" — sets the register in eleven letterspaced words.

ii.Scrolling through the screen

The signature scroll piece: a pinned viewport where the cinema photograph scales from 1× to 3.1× with its transform-origin on the screen itself. As you scroll you physically approach the glow; brightness rises, the caption retires, and at the threshold the shot crossfades into the projector beam — you have passed through the screen into the light. One timeline, five tweens, huge effect.

iii.Theatrical furniture

  • Curtain wipes — every feature image reveals from its centerline outward (inset(0 50% 0 50%) → inset(0 0 0 0)), the same move as the hero curtains.
  • A scrubbed credits roll — a pinned section where end credits (LIGHTING DESIGN — Smart Installers… STARRING — Your Family) roll bottom-to-top under your scroll control, with cinematic fade masks top and bottom.
  • NOW SHOWING marquee in Prata, speed tied to scroll velocity.
  • A permanent candlelight vignette over the whole page, and gold reserved strictly for light sources: text glows only where the room would.

iv.The stack & the recipe

  • Static HTML, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Lenis. Prata display, Cormorant italic voice, Karla body. All self-hosted.
  • Photography generated with Higgsfield to one candle-lit brief; hero animated with Kling.
  • To repeat: borrow a ritual everyone already knows (a night at the cinema) and translate each beat into a scroll mechanic — arrival, reveal, immersion, credits. The structure writes the animation list for you.
  • Reduced-motion visitors get a fully static, readable cut.
