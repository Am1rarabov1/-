The making-of, told plainly

The real homepage, re-set in Atelier

This page took Smart Installers' actual website copy — headline, services, featured projects, client list, stats, phone number, service areas — and re-set every word in the Atelier design system. Nothing about the business was invented; everything about the presentation was. Built autonomously by Claude (Fable 5).

i.Same words, different temperature

The original homepage says it with a corporate blue hero and card grids. The redesign says the identical things — "Security & Automation, Precision Engineered," the six services verbatim, the three real projects, the Fortune 500 client names — but at dusk, in bone and espresso, with candle-amber reserved for what matters. The claim "precision engineered" stops being a slogan and becomes the visual standard the page itself has to meet.

ii.Photography brief: New York at dusk

Every image was generated with Higgsfield's image model to one brief — the Atelier lighting law applied to the company's real subject matter: a prewar facade with windows warming (animated into the hero cinemagraph with Kling 3.0 Turbo), an Upper East Side limestone condo, a Maspeth commercial entrance, a Bed-Stuy brownstone, a discreet dome camera on a limestone corner, a brass video intercom, a lobby access reader, a perfectly dressed rack in amber light. Security photographed as architecture, not as hardware.

iii.The set pieces, repurposed

  • Sticky project lens. Atelier's room chapters became the Featured Projects: the photograph crossfades between the three real installations as their stories scroll past, each closing on its true one-line spec (Upper East Side · Condo · In Progress).
  • The dial became the platform demo. The company's own sentence — "security, access, audio, and automation unified under one intelligent platform" — is now a working interaction: Arrive / Entertain / Secure / Goodnight re-light the lobby photograph live.
  • The 500+ counter rolls up in Cormorant numerals inside the dark Why-Us band, with the three real differentiators set as engraved rows.
  • Trust, letterspaced. Amazon, NYU Langone, Chipotle, Primark, H&M, Uniqlo and Northwell Health appear as an Italiana specimen row rather than a logo soup.

iv.The stack

  • Hand-written static HTML — no framework, no build step. GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText, Lenis smooth scroll, all self-hosted.
  • Italiana / Cormorant Garamond / Karla, self-hosted woff2.
  • All motion collapses politely under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Deploys anywhere static files go: GitHub Pages today, Netlify with netlify deploy --prod --dir smart.

v.If you are redesigning your own site

  1. Freeze the copy first. Rewriting words and design at once is how brands drift.
  2. Pick one lighting brief and regenerate every image with it — the photography carries the palette.
  3. Turn your strongest claim into an interaction (ours: "one intelligent platform" became the dial).
  4. Keep the phone number, the service areas, the real projects. Specificity is what luxury design usually throws away — keeping it is the trick.
